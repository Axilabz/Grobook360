📦 Purchase Chain — Data Flow

Index Sales Purchase Payments Inventory Payroll Accounting/GST Reports
Purchase Order → Goods Receipt → Purchase Invoice → Purchase Return. The mirror image of the Sales chain, with one structural difference: Purchase Invoice has 3 entry paths (Direct, from-Goods-Receipt, from-Purchase-Order-consolidated), and GST tracking here is Input GST/ITC, not Output.

purchase_invoicepurchase_invoice_linegoods_receipt_line

GR/PO-sourced invoices increment goods_receipt_line.invoiced_quantity instead of moving stock.

Side-Effects, by invoice_type
  • DIRECT_PURCHASE_INVOICE: Stock Ledger post_movement(STOCK_IN, source_type=PURCHASE_INVOICE) per line.
  • GOODS_RECEIPT_ / PURCHASE_ORDER_PURCHASE_INVOICE: no stock call — the GR already moved it.

All 3 branches then run:

  1. Accounting AccountingPostingService.post_purchase_invoice() — DR Purchase Expense + Input CGST/SGST/IGST / CR Accounts Payable.
  2. GST GstReportingService.record_purchase_invoice_transaction() (at purchase_invoice_service.py:805) — Input GST/ITC, positive sign. Same GstReportingService class the Sales chain uses (app/services/sales/gst_reporting_service.py), not a separate GST module.
  3. Audit AuditService.record("PURCHASE_INVOICE_POSTED")
↓ Cancelling a POSTED invoice reverses it: Direct invoices post a compensating PURCHASE_INVOICE_REVERSAL Stock-Out; GR/PO-sourced ones only release their invoiced_quantity claim, never touching stock
