🧑‍🔧 A Day in the Life — Salesperson

All Roles Owner Manager Salesperson

You're out in the field, visiting customers.

Your day is about customers — visiting them, taking their orders, billing them, and collecting payment. The bigger approvals (dispatching stock, posting the final bill) go to your Manager or Owner, but you handle everything up to that point yourself, right from your phone. Click any step for a bit more detail.

🔑 You log in

You open the app on your phone before heading out — phone number and password, and you're in.

Morning
🏠You check your own numbers

A simpler dashboard — your sales, stock levels, what's happening today.

Your home screen is a bit simpler than your Manager's — it's focused on sales and stock, not company finances. You can see today's activity and jump straight into creating a new invoice or order.

📍You pull up a customer before visiting them

See their details, their order history, even get directions to their shop.

You search for the customer, see everything about them in one place — past orders, what they owe — and if their location is saved, you can tap to get directions straight to their door. If you're visiting someone new, you add them to the system on the spot.

📝You prepare a quotation

If they're still deciding, you give them a price list to think over.

You pick the products they're interested in, the app works out the pricing and tax automatically, and you can send it to them to review before they commit.

Midday
🛒You take their order

They're ready to buy — you log what they want.

You create the order right there with the customer. It then goes to your Manager or Owner to confirm, which is what tells the warehouse it's ready to be packed.

Your Manager or Owner gives the final confirmation before it's packed
🚚You prepare the delivery note

If you're handling the delivery side too, you note down what's going out.

You put together the delivery note for the order. Your Manager or Owner confirms it when it actually leaves — that's the moment stock officially goes down.

🧾You bill the customer

You put together the invoice — your Manager or Owner finalizes it.

You prepare the invoice with all the right products and pricing. Posting it — making it official and starting the payment clock — is the last step your Manager or Owner takes.

Afternoon
💵You collect payment on the spot

The customer pays you right there — you log it immediately.

Whether it's cash, a bank transfer, or a cheque, you record the payment immediately against their invoice, right from your phone — no need to wait until you're back at the office.

↩️You handle a return, if needed

Something's damaged or wrong — you note it down.

If a customer needs to return something, you log it with a reason. Your Manager or Owner confirms it, which is what brings the stock back in.

📶No signal? No problem.

If you're somewhere with bad network, you can still work.

You can still create an invoice or add a customer even when you're offline — it's saved on your phone and automatically sent through the moment you're back online, so you never lose a sale because of signal.

📈You check how you're doing

Your own sales numbers — not the whole company's.

You can see your own sales performance — how many invoices, how much you've sold. You see your own numbers, not what everyone else on the team is doing.

You see your own performance, not the whole team's

🌙 You log out

End of your rounds, you log out. Everything you logged today is saved and visible to your Manager and Owner right away.

Every step here is a real, working part of the app — just explained without the technical details.
