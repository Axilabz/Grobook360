🧑‍💼 A Day in the Life — Manager

All Roles Owner Manager Salesperson

You run the day-to-day. You can do almost everything the Owner can.

You handle sales, purchases, payments, and your team's work — the Owner trusts you to keep things moving. A handful of things (adding new staff, changing company-wide settings) stay with the Owner, but everything else is yours to run. Click any step for a bit more detail.

🔑 You log in

Phone number and password, and you're straight into your dashboard.

Morning
🏠You check what needs attention today

Today's sales, stock running low, money owed — the same dashboard the Owner sees.

You see the same full picture as the Owner: sales so far today, what customers owe, what's running low, recent activity. Anything that needs action is flagged.

✅You confirm orders your salespeople created

Your field team took orders — you give the go-ahead so they can be fulfilled.

Your salespeople can create quotations and orders, but you're the one who confirms them, which is what actually kicks off packing and delivery.

🚚You confirm deliveries and post invoices

Goods go out, invoices go out — both need your sign-off.

You confirm that a delivery has actually gone out (this is the moment stock leaves), and you post the invoice for it, which makes it official and starts the customer's payment clock.

👤You keep the customer list up to date

Adding new customers, updating details, keeping things tidy.

You can add, edit, and even temporarily deactivate a customer or supplier record, so the team's lists always stay accurate.

Midday
💰You handle the money — collections and payments

A customer pays you, you pay a supplier — you record both.

You record money coming in from customers and going out to suppliers, and settle it against the right invoices. If a cheque bounces, you mark it and the payment is automatically reversed.

📦You handle purchasing and restocking

Order from suppliers, receive goods, post their bills.

You place and confirm purchase orders, check goods in when they arrive (that's when your stock count goes up), and post the supplier's invoice so it's reflected in what you owe them.

Afternoon
📈You check in on performance and the books

Reports, analysis, and a look at the accounting ledger.

You can see sales and purchase reports, how the team's doing, and browse the accounting ledger to see how transactions have been recorded. A couple of the more sensitive accounting actions (like undoing a posted entry) stay with the Owner — you can view, but not reverse.

A few higher-risk financial actions are Owner-only
🧾You keep an eye on GST

You can see the GST numbers and help prepare the filing.

You can view GST totals and help get the return ready. Exporting the final filing data is something only the Owner does.

👥You see the team list

You can see who's on the team — adding someone new is the Owner's call.

You can view the staff list, but inviting a new person or changing someone's role is reserved for the Owner.

Adding staff and changing company settings stay Owner-only

🌙 You log out

End of shift, you log out. Everything's saved and waiting for you next time.

Every step here is a real, working part of the app — just explained without the technical details.
