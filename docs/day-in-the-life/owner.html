👑 A Day in the Life — Owner

All Roles Owner Manager Salesperson

You own the business. You see everything.

You're the only one who can add new staff, change business settings, and have the final say on money matters. Here's what a normal day looks like, start to finish. Click any step for a bit more detail.

🔑 You log in

You open the app on your phone or computer and enter your phone number and password. Within a second you're looking at your business's home screen — no setup, no waiting.

Morning
🏠You check the Dashboard first

A quick snapshot of yesterday and today, before you do anything else.

Right away you see: how much you sold today, how much money customers still owe you, how much you owe your suppliers, and whether anything is running low or out of stock. If something needs your attention — like a big payment that's overdue — it's flagged right at the top.

✅You approve the orders your team lined up

Your salespeople took orders yesterday — you give them the green light.

Your sales staff can create quotations and sales orders for customers, but the final "go ahead, let's fulfill this" is yours (or your manager's). You glance through what came in overnight, confirm the ones that look right, and they're now ready to be packed and sent out.

Only you or your manager can give this final confirmation
🚚You sign off on deliveries and billing

Once goods actually go out the door, and once an invoice is ready to send.

When a delivery is packed and ready to leave, you confirm it — that's the moment stock actually leaves your warehouse. And when it's time to bill the customer, you post the invoice — that's what makes it official and starts the clock on getting paid.

Midday
💰You record money coming in and going out

A customer pays you, or you pay a supplier — both get logged here.

When a customer pays (cash, bank transfer, UPI, or cheque), you record it and it automatically settles against whichever invoice(s) it's for. Same thing in reverse when you pay a supplier. Every payment is tracked — if a cheque bounces, you can mark that too, and it automatically undoes the payment.

📦You restock — order from suppliers, receive goods

When something's running low, you order more — and check it in when it arrives.

You place a purchase order with your supplier, confirm it, and when the delivery truck shows up, you check the goods in. That's the moment your stock count actually goes up. Later, you post the supplier's bill so your books reflect what you owe them.

📈You take a look at how business is going

A few minutes checking the numbers that matter.

You pull up reports — this week vs last week, who your top customers are, which products are selling fastest, who on your team is bringing in the most sales. You can also see a running record of every sale, purchase, and payment, book-keeping style, so you always know where you stand.

Afternoon
🧾You keep an eye on tax (GST)

A quick check that the GST numbers add up before filing time.

Every sale and purchase automatically contributes to your GST totals behind the scenes. You can check how much GST you've collected vs paid, and get the figures ready for your monthly filing without having to calculate anything by hand.

👥You manage your team

Only you can add a new staff member or change someone's access.

If you hire someone new, you add them here and decide if they're a Manager or a Salesperson — that decides what they'll be able to see and do. If it's payday, you also run payroll from here and finalize it once everything looks right.

This part of the app is for you only — your Manager can't do this
⚙️You tweak how things look and work

The small stuff — your company profile, how invoices look when printed.

You can update your company's details (address, bank info, logo) and customize how your printed invoices look — your logo, your colors, what information shows up on them.

🌙 You log out

At the end of the day, you log out. Everything you did is saved — tomorrow when you log back in, your dashboard picks up right where today left off.

Every step here is a real, working part of the app — just explained without the technical details.
